THE COUNTRY POPUP, UP CLOSE

One glance. Then the detail.

A compact hover card gives the essentials. Clicking opens the full card: fandom and trend first, fan groups next, then league health and rival pressure. The popup treatment changes with each world.

From your reference. I kept direct country hover, immediate numbers, and click-to-inspect. The simple tooltip and definition list become compact cards with a clearer reading order, small trend charts, fan tiles and distinct league/rival sections.

What works here. Switch looks, hover, pin and close stats, pan, zoom, locate a market, or scrub recorded turns. These are lightweight art studies using the same Senegal campaign data.
