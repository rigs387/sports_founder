What this is. A mock-up of the map screen, built to see whether watching the colour bleed outward is worth building properly. The campaign is real: seed 7, the backyard-kickball preset, anchored in Slovakia, played by the builder bot through turn 220 — year 2169 in game. It reaches tier 3 around turn 70, passes cricket into second place by turn 110, and takes first place from soccer before turn 160.

What this is not. The geography is a stand-in. Every market gets an equal tile, continents sit in roughly their familiar positions, and within a continent the markets are ordered breadth-first across their real borders and sea links, so neighbours land near each other. No coordinates were invented for it. The real build reads Natural Earth shapes, which is the first task in docs/world-map-brief.html — and equal tiles quietly solve a problem real shapes will not: Tuvalu, 9,646 people and the game’s signature hard anchor, is as clickable here as China.

Regenerate with npx tsx runs/demo-map.ts <anchor> <seed>, which rewrites world-map-example.data.js beside this file.
